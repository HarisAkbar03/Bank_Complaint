**Assisted Complaint Resolution Journey Map**

**Purpose**

This journey's purpose is to map out the future AI-assisted complaint workflows for the banking resolution system. It highlights where the AI assistant and automation tools accelerate the investigation process for database queries, policy lookups, and document retrieval, while preserving human control over final decision authority, and accountability.

**Assisted Journey Map**

| **Spec Flow**                           | **Actor/ System**      | **Action**                                                     | **Action Performed & State Transition**                                                                                                                   | **Data & Automation Layer**                                                                                                                               | **Human Control**                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1\. Case Initialization & Ingestion     | Customer/System        | Receives the complaint and reviews customer details.           | **Happy Path:** Complaint is submitted-> Record Created-> Info is Validated<br><br>**State:** Submitted-> Intake Complete.                                | **SQL:** Ingests baseline complaint metadata, customer identifiers, and timestamps.<br><br>Automatically ingests complaint details to make a base record. | System flags missing information.                                                                                                                |
| 2\. Automated Triage & Policy Retrieval | Analyst & AI System    | Reviews account and transaction history.                       | **Happy Path:** Complaint retrieved->policies retrieved-> Missing info identified<br><br>**State:** Intake Complete-> Ready for Investigation             | **SQL:** Retrieve customer/account history<br><br>**RAG:** Takes approved policy docs.                                                                    | System finds conditions requiring escalation of business rules.                                                                                  |
| 3\. Analyst Review & Modification       | Complaint Analyst & AI | Collects evidence and checks bank policies.                    | **Happy Path:** Analyst reviews collected-> Review AI recommendations->Update details<br><br>**State:** Ready for Investigation -> Pending Review         | **Generated Assistance:** recommendations made with source attribution                                                                                    | Interface separates Verified facts from System Analysis<br><br>Analysts cannot approve fee reversals, final remedies, or financial compensation. |
| 4\. Human Review & Approval Gate        | Supervisor/Reviewer    | Prepares a recommended solution based on the evidence.         | **Decision:** Reviewer looks at findings to either approve, reject, modify, or escalate.<br><br>**State:** Pending Review->Approve, Reject, or Escalated. | Display audit history, policy citations, and notes to reviewers.                                                                                          | Outcome requires human authorization.<br><br>Requires overrides.                                                                                 |
| 5\. Final Resolution & Audit Logging    | Supervisor & System    | Reviews and approves, rejects, or modifies the recommendation. | **Happy Path:** Final decision recorded->Audit->Complaint closed<br><br>**State:** Approved-> Closed                                                      | Update system state and archive audit logs                                                                                                                | The assistant does not talk to customers directly. Cases will be closed off until supervisor sign-off is logged.                                 |
