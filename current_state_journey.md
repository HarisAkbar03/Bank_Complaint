# **Current State Complaint Journey**
## **Purpose**
The purpose of this journey is to explain how customer complaints are currently handled without AI assistance. It also shows the responsibilities of the analyst and supervisor, along with possible delays in the process.
## **Current Complaint Process**


| **Step** | **Role** | **Action** |
| :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 1. Complaint Intake | Analyst | Receives the complaint and reviews customer details. |
| 2. Investigation | Analyst | Reviews account and transaction history. |
| 3. Evidence Search | Analyst | Collects evidence and checks bank policies. |
| 4. Recommendation | Analyst | Prepares a recommended solution based on the evidence. |
| 5. Approval | Supervisor | Reviews and approves, rejects, or modifies the recommendation. |
| 6. Customer Response | Not specified | The approved decision is communicated to the customer. |
| 7. Case Closure | Supervisor | Closes the approved complaint case. |
## **Systems / Information Used**
- **Complaint Records:** Customer complaints and related details.

- **Customer Records:** Account and transaction history.

- **Policy Documents:** Bank policies used during investigations.

- **Investigation Notes:** Evidence and recommendations collected by analysts.

- **Final Decision:** Approved outcome used for response and closure.
## **Handoffs**
- **Analyst to Supervisor:** The analyst sends the completed investigation and recommendation to the supervisor.

- **Supervisor to Analyst:** The supervisor may send the case back if more evidence or information is needed.

- **Supervisor to Closure:** Once the complaint is approved, the supervisor can close the case.
## **Main Pain Points**
- Searching customer records manually can take time.

- Finding the correct evidence and bank policies may slow down investigations.

- Missing information can cause delays.

- Analysts may have to repeat work if supervisors request more information.

- Waiting for approval can delay the complaint resolution.
## **Summary**
The current complaint process involves analysts reviewing complaints, collecting evidence and preparing recommendations. Supervisors then review the information and make the final decision. Some delays may happen when information is missing or additional investigation is needed.
